## Release decision

The post-0.1.3 repair accepts OSC 0/1/2 title payloads containing semicolons,
retains SGR blink and hidden cell attributes, and retains DEC whole-screen
reverse video as a separate screen mode. Incremental and full paint emit the
corresponding controls. The public Session API, wire format, production
dependencies, resource limits, and system-title policy do not change.

Mouse modes 1001/1015, malformed OSC 52, more than eight scalars per cell,
and overlarge encoded cells still fail explicitly. Raw ST and split zero-width
host-byte boundaries remain separate investigations. The
[batch compatibility fixture](../fixtures/stock-1.4.0-terminal-compatibility-batch.md)
records the case matrix and the black-box stock-server result. xterm.js does
not promise visible blinking or DEC ?5 screen inversion; the library preserves
these VT controls but the consumer controls their rendering.
